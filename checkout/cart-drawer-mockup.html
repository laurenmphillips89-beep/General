The two-step reward ladder

Most orders right now are one bath product or a crayons-and-rocks pair, sitting around $40. With goals at $75 and $100, most carts need two more products to reach free shipping. That makes the progress bar and the slider do more of the work.

$75
Free shipping 🚚Almost double your current AOV. A $40 cart gets there with a mat ($24.95) plus Bee-Long crayons ($19.95), or with a Colourful Adventure kit ($44.95).
$100
Free 8-pack of washable markers 🎁Retail value $9.95, so the gift feels real, and it's useful with a mat. If you'd rather keep markers for paying customers, swap in a free printable colouring pack.

Why two goals and not one: once a mum unlocks free shipping, the bar shouldn't stop there. A second reward a few dollars away keeps her adding. Show the next goal straight away ("Yay, free shipping! Add $19.20 for a free marker pack").

What goes in the cart, top to bottom

  1. Progress bar first. It sits above the items so it's the first thing she reads. One line of copy that always names the exact dollar gap.
  2. Cart items, with the free gift auto-added as a line item marked Free and the $9.95 crossed out. Seeing the value makes it stick.
  3. Bundle swap prompt. When the cart holds items that make up a bundle, offer the bundle as a one-tap swap. It's an honest saving and a higher-value product.
  4. "Little ones also love" slider. 3–4 swipeable cards that change with the cart, each with a one-tap Add button. No leaving the drawer.
  5. Sticky footer: subtotal, a big Forest Green checkout button, Sydney-made trust badges and one short review.

How to build it on your Shopify plan

  • The checkout page itself can't be redesigned on Shopify Basic. Adding upsells or blocks inside the Information, Shipping and Payment steps needs Shopify Plus. That's why the design above lives in the slide-out cart.
  • Cart drawer app: UpCart, Kaching Cart or Rebuy Smart Cart all do the progress bar with tiers, the auto-added gift, and the rules-based slider. UpCart is the easiest to set up and the cheapest.
  • Free shipping: set it in Shopify (Settings → Shipping, free rate on orders over $75) so it applies at checkout and isn't only a cart-drawer promise.
  • Free gift: make a $0 "Free Marker Pack" variant (hidden from search) and have the app auto-add it at $100 and remove it if the cart drops below.
  • After checkout: a one-click post-purchase offer (e.g. AfterSell) works on every plan. Offer Replacement Pens or a second mat at 15% off.
  • Test it: run it for 4 weeks and compare Online Store AOV and conversion rate against the $39.35 baseline.
